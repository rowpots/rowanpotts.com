About

I’m Rowan Potts, a photographer based in Harrisonburg, Virginia.

I shoot JMU sports, concerts and events, and my work has appeared in The Breeze, JMU’s award-winning student newspaper.

I also photograph portraits and graduations around Harrisonburg and Northern Virginia.

Off the sideline, I’m a computer science student at James Madison University.
